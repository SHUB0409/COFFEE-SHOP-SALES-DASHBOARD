# COFFEE-SHOP-SALES-DASHBOARD
# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

## 🧩 Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance for a coffee shop chain using Excel and Tableau dashboards.  
The goal is to identify trends in **sales, profit, and customer behavior** to support data-driven business decisions.  
It focuses on visualizing performance across **locations, products, and time periods**.

---

## 📊 Objectives
- Analyze total sales and profit across different coffee shop locations.  
- Identify top-performing products and categories.  
- Track monthly and daily sales trends to understand peak business periods.  
- Evaluate sales by customer segment to support targeted marketing.  

---

## 📁 Dataset
**File:** `coffeshop sales dashboard.xlsx`  
This Excel workbook contains detailed transactional data including:
- `Date` – Transaction date  
- `Product Category` – Beverage or food category  
- `Location` – Coffee shop branch or region  
- `Quantity Sold` – Units sold per transaction  
- `Total Sales` – Revenue generated per sale  
- `Profit` – Net profit from each sale  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Summary
The interactive dashboard visualizes the following KPIs and trends:
1. **Total Sales & Profit Overview:**  
   Shows overall business performance with total revenue and profit margins.  
2. **Sales by Product Category:**  
   Highlights top-selling coffee, tea, and snack items.  
3. **Sales by Location:**  
   Compares performance across coffee shop branches to identify best-performing outlets.  
4. **Monthly Sales Trend:**  
   Displays seasonality and growth patterns in monthly revenue.  
5. **Top Products & Profit Drivers:**  
   Lists the most profitable products and their contribution to total sales.

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, transformation, and chart creation.  
- **Tableau Desktop** – Interactive dashboard design and visualization.  
- **Techniques Used:** Data aggregation, KPI analysis, trend visualization, and dashboard storytelling.  

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the dataset: `coffeshop sales dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Excel** or **Tableau**.  
3. Explore pivot tables or charts that show:
   - Sales by location and product category  
   - Profit and quantity sold trends  
4. Interact with dashboard filters (if using Tableau) to analyze by month or region.
